# Sources

Numbers in the game are simplified so kids can do the math. These are the real-world facts behind them.

## Science

| Used in the game | Source |
|---|---|
| Ice hides in dark craters near the Moon's south pole | [NASA NSSDC: Ice on the Moon](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/ice/ice_moon.html) |
| MOXIE made oxygen from Mars air (the game's Oxygen Maker) | [MIT AeroAstro: MOXIE completes Mars mission](https://aeroastro.mit.edu/news-impact/oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission) |
| Mars and Earth line up for launch about every 26 months | [Launch window (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Launch_window) |
| Airbags landed small, light rovers on Mars | [NASA JPL: Mars Exploration Rover airbag installation](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia04852-air-bag-installation/) |
| Astronauts grew and ate lettuce in space (the Greenhouse) | [Scientific American: astronauts take first bites of lettuce grown in space](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/astronauts-take-first-bites-of-lettuce-grown-in-space/) |
| Juno protects its electronics in a titanium radiation vault | [NASA JPL: Juno armored up to go to Jupiter](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/juno-armored-up-to-go-to-jupiter/) |
| Europa Clipper loops around Jupiter and flies past Europa, instead of staying in the worst radiation | [NASA JPL: 8 things to know about Europa Clipper](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/8-things-to-know-about-nasas-mission-to-an-ocean-moon-of-jupiter/) · [Europa Clipper's elliptical orbit](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia26436-europa-clippers-elliptical-orbit/) |
| The James Webb Space Telescope studies TRAPPIST-1e's atmosphere | [STScI news release 2025-109](https://www.stsci.edu/contents/news-releases/2025/news-2025-109) |
| Lunar day and night (about 14 Earth days each), weak sunlight at Jupiter, message travel times, Proxima b's distance | [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/) |

The Mystery Planet clue cards are pretend. No planet beyond Earth has confirmed signs of life.
