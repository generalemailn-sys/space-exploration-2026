# Space Exploration 2026: Game Design

**Current as of November 2026.**

A decision game about thinking ahead. The player picks a destination, then plays 4 rounds. Each round asks a question, offers choices, and shows the consequence and the reason for it.

## How a mission goes

1. **Mission Academy** (first time only, skippable): your job, the 5 things you juggle, how a mission goes.
2. **Team:** 1–4 players. Each player gets a role: Mission Planner, Spacecraft Engineer, Science Officer, Life Support & Comms.
3. **Briefing:** challenges, things to think about, and a choice of 2 objectives (each splits the 100 points differently).
4. **Round 1 Research:** pick a site (Jupiter: a moon, then an orbit).
5. **Round 2 Prepare:** the shop. Parts first, rocket last. "Ready to launch?" checklist, Simulate (first test free, then 1 coin), team check before launch.
6. **Round 3 Explore:** surprise events.
7. **Round 4 Sustain:** an animated story that stops only when the player must decide.
8. **Score** out of 100, Engineer's tips, Mission report (copyable).

Helpers on every screen: "Your job now" box (Do / Why, can be hidden), Read to me, Space words, How to play, Clues (3 per round, including the briefing), Quit, Mission tally and My choices side panels. Back buttons only before launch. Sandbox mode: unlimited coins, Simulate free. Mission records on this computer, with a Clear records button.

## Mission rules (all planets)

1. Everything must fit your coins and your rocket's "can carry" number.
2. Power made must be at least power used. If it drops, switch machines off; the Habitat or Robot Body always stays on. While switching, a live preview shows the water, air and food result, and warns if the Drill or Melter is on alone.
3. Round down. Unused water stays in the tank.
4. When a machine is lost, the player chooses which (never the Habitat, the landing system or anything in orbit).
5. If the crew runs out of water or air, they go home and the mission ends.
6. Mars launch window: the first time a plan that doesn't fit is launched, it's a warning; the second time costs the 10 "On time" points.

## Planet summaries

**Moon: Build the First Base.** Sites: South Pole rim (4 water), Equator (3 water, only 2 events), Far Side (3 water, telescope +10, needs a Relay Satellite). Nuclear +5 power; Battery +3 at night (needs a solar panel). Events: Boulder Field (pay 1 coin for a photo or roll), Sticky Moon Dust, Solar Flare (−1 water that day), Lucky Ice Pocket. Sustain: Lunar Day, then Lunar Night. Crew needs 3 water a turn; extra water can become rocket fuel.

**Mars: Live on Another Planet.** Sites: Pole (5 water), Middle (4), Equator (1 water, +1 power per solar panel). Landing: Parachute + Rockets (any mass) or Airbags (mass 8 or less); without a **Landing Radar** (2 coins, mass 1), roll a die at landing and a 1 breaks a machine. Events: Seven Minutes of Terror (always), plus 2 of Rover Near a Cliff, Dusty Panels, Ancient Riverbed. Seasons: Spring, Summer (dust storms), Autumn (recycler may break), Winter (cold). Food: a Food Crate per season, or a Greenhouse (food from Season 2) plus a crate for Spring.

**Jupiter: Robots to the Ocean Moons.** Robot Health 11. Moons: Europa (6 points per data packet, radiation 2 a year), Ganymede (5, 1), Callisto (4, 0). Orbits: Far orbit (2 less radiation, 1 less data a year), Loop like Europa Clipper (normal), Moon orbit (1 more radiation, 2 more data, 3 coins and 1 mass of braking fuel). Mission is 4 years. The Year 4 close flyby shows Health after the flyby and after that year's radiation before you choose.

**Mystery Planet: Is Anyone Out There?** Targets: Proxima b, TRAPPIST-1e, Planet X (made-up). Tools draw Air Clues and Planet Clues. Sustain: **sort each clue** (Hint of life: Water Vapor, Oxygen + Methane, Just Right · Not proof: Oxygen, Methane, Carbon Dioxide · Bad news: Too Hot, Star Flares), then the Verdict (Too Hot → No life; Oxygen + Methane, not Too Hot → Strong hint; otherwise Maybe), Pass the Torch, and Probe Arrives (Proxima b only). The clues are pretend, and the game says so.
