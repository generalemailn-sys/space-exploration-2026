    Instructions
    1. Click the green flag. New players: read the 3 Mission Academy screens.
    2. Pick a planet, choose 1–4 explorers, and read the mission briefing.
    3. The yellow Your job now box always says what to do and why.
    4. In the shop, pack parts first, then pick the smallest rocket that fits. Tap Simulate to test (first test free).
    5. Score 60 or more out of 100 to move on. Try Sandbox to practice with unlimited coins.

    Notes and Credits

    Built for the NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge "Space Mission Design Game": design, manage and simulate a space mission while balancing budget, mass, power, data, communications and orbit, alone or as a team of up to 4.

    Real science inside: Moon ice in dark south-pole craters and 14-day lunar nights; Mars launch windows every 26 months and NASA's MOXIE oxygen maker; Jupiter's sunlight about 25 times weaker, Juno's radiation vault and Europa Clipper's looping orbit; the James Webb Space Telescope reading planet atmospheres. The Mystery Planet clues are pretend: no planet beyond Earth has confirmed signs of life.
